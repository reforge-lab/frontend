title: Welcome to Reforge
description: Documentation hub for the MEV-Aware Batch Auction Liquidation Protocol
---

Reforge is an MEV-aware batch auction mechanism for DeFi liquidations. It replaces gas-war Priority Gas Auctions with economic efficiency-based scoring — selecting liquidators by **Net Economic Value** rather than who pays the highest gas bribe.
